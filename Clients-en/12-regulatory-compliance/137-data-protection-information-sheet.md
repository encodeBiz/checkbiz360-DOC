# Data protection information sheet, signed from the app

The law requires the company to inform each person of how their data is processed before they clock in for the first time, and to keep proof of having done so. CheckBiz360 prepares that sheet with your company's details, sends it to each person in the app for them to sign and keeps the proof in their record. If your company already has its own sheet, you can use that one.

---

## What it solves

- **There is nothing to draft**: the sheet comes filled in with the company name, the tax ID and the features your company has turned on.
- **It is sent automatically when a person is added**, with no extra steps.
- **The proof is kept**: who signed it, when and how, with its record, to show it if asked.
- **You know who is missing**: the employee list shows each person's status, and the *Data protection notice not signed* alert reminds you who is missing it.

Signing the sheet is not giving consent or waiving anything: it is recording that the person has received the information.

---

## Step 1: complete your company's details (once)

1. Go to **Entity → Company Data**.
2. In the **Data protection** block, fill in:
   - **Data-protection contact person** and their **Contact email**;
   - **How people clock in at your workplaces**: *With the app on their phone*, *At the clock-in point* or *Both*.
3. Save.

The platform fills in the rest. Below, you will see the version of the sheet text being used.

Until these details are complete, the sending checkbox on the new-employee form appears disabled, with a link to this screen. Adding the person is not blocked.

### If your company has its own sheet

In the same block, **Your company's own information sheet (optional)**:

1. Click **Upload own sheet (PDF)** and choose the file. It has to be a PDF.
2. From then on, each person receives that sheet to sign, instead of the CheckBiz360 one.

You can **View** the sheet in use, **Replace** it with another or **Remove and use the CheckBiz360 sheet**.

---

## Step 2: sending it

### When adding a person

On the employee form, the checkbox **Send the data-protection information sheet to be signed from the app** is ticked by default. When the new employee is saved, the sheet is sent automatically.

If you untick it, the panel reminds you that your company will have to inform that person by other means and keep proof. You can send it later.

### To people who already work with you

1. In **Document Management**, upload a new document.
2. Choose the **Employee** scope and the person.
3. In **Document type**, choose **Data protection information sheet**.
4. Choose **Use the CheckBiz360 sheet** (or **Use my company's sheet**, if you uploaded yours) and click **Send**. No file needs to be uploaded. With **See the sheet before sending it** you can review it first.
   If you prefer to send another document as the information sheet, choose **Upload my own document**: it is sent for signature and recorded as their information sheet.

If the person already has the current version of the sheet, the panel tells you and does not send it again.

---

## What the person does

They receive a notification and the sheet appears in **Documents** in their app, with *Acknowledgement pending*. They open it, read it and sign that they have received it with **Acknowledge receipt**. If their phone has fingerprint or face recognition, the signature is made with it.

---

## Knowing who has signed it

- In the **employee list**, the **Information sheet** column shows for each person *Signed on…*, *Sent on…* or *Not sent*.
- The **document record** shows who acknowledged receipt, when and how, with its record.
- On **Home** and in the morning email summary, the red **Data protection notice not signed** alert says who is missing and how many have not been sent it. See [Email alerts](../09-notifications/133-email-alerts.md).

The first time you see the alert, almost all your staff may appear, if the sheet was never sent. It shrinks as people sign.

> **Warning:** if you delete a person's signed sheet, the proof is lost and the person appears in the alert again. See [Delete documents](../05-document-management/135-delete-documents.md).

---

## Common problems

**The checkbox on the new-employee form is disabled.** Your company's data protection details are missing. Complete them in **Entity → Company Data** (step 1).

**When I choose the type it asks for the "Employee" scope.** The sheet is sent to a specific person: choose the *Employee* scope.

**The person says they cannot see it.** Ask them to open **Documents** in the app. If they did not receive their app access, check the **App access** block on their record. See [App access and email change](../02-employees/116-app-access-and-email-change.md).

**Someone does not want to sign.** CheckBiz360 does not stop them from clocking in. Informing staff is the company's obligation: if they do not sign, inform them by other means and keep proof.
