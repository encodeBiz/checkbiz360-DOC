# Your operations manager in Claude or ChatGPT

Building the week, adding the new hires, moving a job because someone is off sick: it is the work that eats the evenings of whoever runs the operation. If you already use Claude or ChatGPT, you can hand it over. Connect your CheckBiz360 company to your assistant and ask for things in your own words: it does them in CheckBiz360 with your permissions, shows you what it is about to do, and saves nothing until you say yes.

---

## What it solves

- **"Build next week for the North crew like last week, but Marta is off on Thursday."** The assistant prepares the whole week, shows it to you and applies it when you confirm.
- **"Add these twelve people and these three sites."** Paste the list your accountant sent, an Excel file or a photo: the assistant reads it, tells you what it will create, and creates it.
- **"Move the cleaning at 12 Portal Street to Tuesday at ten."** A single change, with before and after in front of you, and the person notified on their phone.
- **"Who is working tomorrow in Alcalá?"** Ask about the week, a site or a person, without opening the panel.

All of this happens with the permissions of whoever connects: no more and no less than what that person can do in the panel.

---

## How it works, in three steps

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| **1. Connect** | Once per company, from the panel. It takes two minutes. |
| **2. Ask** | In Claude or ChatGPT, in your words: "build", "change", "add", "who…?". |
| **3. Review and confirm** | The assistant shows what it is about to do. It only applies it when you say yes. |

---

## 1. Connect your company

1. In the panel, open your company settings and go to the **Workspace** tab.
2. Under **Connect with Claude or ChatGPT**, copy the **connection address for this company**.
3. Follow the steps for the assistant you use:

**In Claude**

1. Open **Settings → Connectors** and click **Add custom connector**.
2. Type a name (for example, *CheckBiz360*), paste the address and click **Add**.
3. Click **Connect**, sign in with your CheckBiz360 user and your verification code, and authorise access.

**In ChatGPT**

1. Open **Settings → Apps and connectors** and click **Create**. If it is not there, first enable developer mode under **Advanced settings**.
2. Type a name, paste the address as the server URL, choose **OAuth** authentication and confirm.
3. Sign in with your CheckBiz360 user and your verification code, and authorise access.

Each company has its own address. If you manage several, add one connector per company and name it after the company so you do not mix them up.

> **Before you start:** you need two-factor authentication enabled and owner or administrator permissions in the company. If the connection will not let you in, it is almost always one of those two.

---

## 2. What you can ask for

There are no commands to learn. Describe what you need as you would to someone on your team. Some of the things it already does:

### See how the week looks

Who works each day and where, which tasks each person has, which sites there are. Ask about a day, a person or a site.

### Build the whole week

- **Copy last week**, with the changes you tell it.
- **From scratch**, dictating the jobs.
- **From a document**: the rota your management software prints, an Excel file, a photo of the paper. The assistant reads it, recognises your people and turns it into each person's week.

In all three cases it shows you the proposal person by person, with the warnings worth a look (two jobs that overlap, a day that is a holiday for someone), and a link to an Excel file with everything, to review calmly. Each person gets their week on their phone, with their hours and the address of each job, only when you confirm.

If the week changes, ask it to propose it again with the right version: what was already there is updated and nothing is duplicated. Jobs the person had already started stay.

### Single changes

Create a task, move it to another day or time, hand it to someone else, cancel it. Also change someone's standard week. Before touching anything it shows you before and after and waits for your yes.

### Add people and sites

One person or one site, or many at once from your accountant's list, an Excel file or a document. The assistant checks who already exists, tells you what it will create and which address it understood for each site, and creates everything when you confirm. The app welcome is not sent on its own: ask for it once you have checked everything is right.

### And whatever comes next

The assistant keeps learning to do more without you touching anything. If you are not sure whether you can ask for something, ask it: it will tell you what it can do in your company.

---

## 3. What protects you

- **Nothing is saved without your yes.** Anything that changes the operation is proposed first and applied when you confirm. If you close the conversation without confirming, nothing happens.
- **It acts with your permissions.** It cannot do anything you could not do in the panel.
- **It does not guess who you mean.** If a name is unclear or two people look alike, it asks, showing each person's email.
- **Times are the site's times.** "At ten" means ten in the branch's time zone, even if you are somewhere else.

---

## Privacy

When you connect, your employees' names and your operational data are sent to the Claude or ChatGPT account you use. It should be a company account, not a personal one, and check in that service what it does with your data. You can remove the connector whenever you like from Claude or ChatGPT: from that moment the assistant loses access.

---

## Common problems

| What happens | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Create** does not appear in ChatGPT | Enable developer mode under **Advanced settings** and go back to **Apps and connectors**. |
| The connection will not let me in | Check that two-factor authentication is enabled and that you are an owner or administrator of that company. |
| It says it cannot find a person | The record may use a different name. Give it that person's email. |
| It proposes but does not apply | Say it clearly: "confirm", "apply it". It makes no changes without an explicit yes. |
| I manage several companies and it mixes them up | Each company has its own connector. Check which one is active in the conversation. |

---

## Who can connect

Owners and administrators of the company with two-factor authentication enabled.
