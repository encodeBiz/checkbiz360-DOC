# Downloading and installing CheckBiz360

CheckBiz360 is available free of charge for iOS and Android devices. This guide tells you how to find the application in each official store, the minimum requirements your device has to meet and the steps needed to complete the installation correctly.

---

## Minimum device requirements

Before starting the download, check that your device meets the following requirements:

| Platform | Minimum operating system version |
|------------|--------------------------------------|
| iOS (iPhone / iPad) | iOS 13 or later |
| Android | Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or later |

In addition to the operating system version, the application requires:

- **GPS / location enabled**: needed for geolocation clocking validations.
- **Push notifications enabled**: to receive task, document and system alerts.
- **An active internet connection** at the moment of clocking in: Wi-Fi or mobile data (4G/5G recommended).
- **Free space on the device**: a minimum of 150 MB available is recommended for installation and caching data.

---

## Downloading on iOS (App Store)

1. Open the **App Store** application on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap the search icon (magnifying glass) in the bottom bar.
3. Type **CheckBiz360** in the search field and press return.
4. Find the **EncodeBiz** application in the results. The app's official App Store identifier is `id6737189938`.
5. Tap the **Get** button and authenticate with your Face ID, Touch ID or Apple ID password when prompted.
6. Wait for the download and installation to finish. When the button changes to **Open**, the installation is complete.
7. Tap **Open** to launch the application for the first time.

> **Warning:** If the button shows **Update** instead of **Get**, it means you already have an earlier version installed. Tap **Update** to get the most recent version before continuing.

---

## Downloading on Android (Google Play)

1. Open the **Google Play Store** application on your Android device.
2. Tap the search bar at the top.
3. Type **CheckBiz360** and tap the search icon.
4. Select the application published by **EncodeBiz** in the results.
5. Tap the **Install** button.
6. Accept the permissions requested (location, notifications, storage) when the system asks for them during or after installation.
7. Once installation is complete, tap **Open** to access the application.

> **Warning:** On some Android devices with customisation layers (Samsung One UI, MIUI, etc.), the system may ask you to confirm background location permissions separately. Make sure you grant the location permission with the "Always" or "While using the app" option to guarantee clocking works correctly.

---

## Permissions required after installation

Once the application is installed, it will request the following permissions:

1. **Location**: select "Allow while using the app" or "Always allow" depending on the options available in your operating system.
2. **Notifications**: accept to receive task, document and system alerts.
3. **Camera** (optional): needed if your company uses photo-based clocking.

---

## Troubleshooting common problems

### The application does not appear in the store

- Check that the store (App Store or Google Play) is correctly configured with an active account.
- Verify that your device meets the minimum operating system version requirements stated at the start of this article.
- On the App Store, make sure your account region is set to a country where the app is available.
- If the problem persists, ask your manager for the direct link to the app's page in the relevant store.

### The device does not meet the compatibility requirements

If your device does not support the minimum operating system version required, the store will tell you the app is not compatible. In that case, check with your organisation's administrator to explore alternative attendance recording options available from the web portal.

### The installation stops or takes too long

- Make sure you have enough free space on the device.
- Check the stability of your internet connection.
- Restart the device and try the installation again.
