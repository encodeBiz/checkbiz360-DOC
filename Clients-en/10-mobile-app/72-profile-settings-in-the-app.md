# Profile settings in the app

The CheckBiz360 profile section lets you personalise your experience in the application and keep your contact details and security preferences up to date. This article describes in detail what you can configure from this section, how to do it and which data only your organisation's administrator or manager can modify.

---

## Accessing the profile section

1. Open CheckBiz360 on your device and sign in.
2. Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar (usually represented by a person silhouette or your initials).
3. You will see the main profile screen with your current information and the various configuration options available.

---

## Contact details

From the profile section you can update certain contact details that are not directly linked to your corporate identity:

### Phone number

1. Tap the **Contact phone** or **Edit details** option.
2. Enter your new phone number.
3. Tap **Save**.

### Profile photo

1. Tap your current photo or the space reserved for the profile image.
2. Select the **Take photo** option to use the device's camera, or **Choose from gallery** to select an existing image.
3. Crop the image if the system asks you to and confirm the selection.
4. The new photo will be updated automatically on your profile.

---

## Changing the password

To modify your access password:

1. Tap the **Change password** or **Security** option.
2. Enter your current password in the first field.
3. Enter the new password in the second field, making sure it meets the security requirements shown on screen.
4. Confirm the new password in the third field.
5. Tap **Save** or **Confirm**.

> **Warning:** If you do not remember your current password, sign out and use the "Forgotten your password?" option on the sign-in screen to reset it through your email address.

---

## Application language

From the profile preferences you can change the language of the application's interface:

1. Tap **Language** or **Language preferences**.
2. Select the language you want from the list available.
3. The application will update immediately to the selected language.

---

## Push notification preferences

You can manage which types of notification you want to receive:

1. Tap the **Notifications** option inside the profile section.
2. Review the categories available: new documents, assigned tasks, clocking reminders, system announcements, among others.
3. Enable or disable each category according to your preferences by tapping the corresponding switch.
4. Changes are saved automatically.

> **Warning:** Disabling document notifications may mean you are not alerted when your organisation publishes documents requiring your read receipt. Keeping at least the document and task notifications enabled is recommended.

---

## Two-step authentication (2FA)

Two-step authentication adds an extra layer of security to your account. To enable it:

1. Tap **Security** and then **Two-step authentication**.
2. Select your preferred verification method: email or authentication application (TOTP).
3. Follow the steps shown on screen to link the selected method.
4. Once enabled, every time you sign in on an unrecognised device you will have to enter the additional verification code.

### Registering trusted devices

After completing two-step verification on a device, you can mark it as a **trusted device** so that you do not have to repeat the process on future sign-ins from that same device:

1. On completing the verification, tick the **Remember this device** or **Mark as trusted device** box.
2. The device will be registered and the additional verification will only be requested again if you sign out manually or the expiry period configured by your organisation elapses.

---

## Biometrics configuration

If your device has a fingerprint sensor or facial recognition, and your organisation has enabled this feature, you can activate biometric access to sign in more quickly:

1. Tap **Security** and then **Biometric access** or **Biometrics**.
2. The application will ask you to confirm your identity with your current password once.
3. Then follow the operating system's instructions to register your biometrics if you have not already done so at device level.
4. Once enabled, you will be able to sign in with your fingerprint or Face ID without having to enter the password manually.

> **Warning:** The availability of biometric authentication depends on the configuration set by your organisation's administrator. If you do not see this option, check with your manager or administrator.

---

## Data the employee cannot modify

Certain data on your profile can only be modified by your organisation's administrator or manager, since it is linked to your account's corporate configuration:

- **Full name and surnames**
- **Email address** (used as the access identifier)
- **Assigned role** (employee, manager or others)
- **Assigned branch or workplace**
- **Assigned schedule and shifts**

If you need to modify any of these details, request it directly from your administrator or manager through your organisation's internal channels.

---

## Role note

This article is aimed at **employees** with access to the CheckBiz360 app. Administrators have additional user management options in the web panel that are not covered in this article.
