# Add customers

The customer record brings together in one place what is usually scattered today: where they are, whom to talk to, what you need to know on arrival and which branches serve them. It is filled in once and every one of their tasks makes use of it.

You will find it under **Customers**, in the side menu.

---

## What problems it solves

- **One place for the customer's details**, instead of emails and loose spreadsheets.
- **Arriving prepared.** Field instructions (keys, doorbell, opening hours) travel with each of the customer's tasks.
- **Choosing correctly when creating a task.** In each branch, only the customers it serves are shown.
- **Finding them in seconds**, by name, tax ID or their code in your invoicing software.

---

## Create a customer

1. In **Customers**, press **New customer**.
2. Choose the **Type**: *Company* or *Person*.
3. Enter the **Name**. It is the only required field, together with the branch.
4. If you wish, add the **Tax ID** and the **Reference**: their code in your invoicing software, to cross-check the data without doubt.
5. In **Branches** (or whatever your company calls its sites), choose at least one: those that serve this customer. When a task is created in them, this customer can be chosen.
6. In **Address**, type the customer's address and **pick it from the list** of suggestions. It is their home or their head office, and it is the one that can later be used as the work site of their tasks.
7. Decide whether to tick **Show the customer's name to staff** (see below).
8. Add the **Contact people**: name, role or relationship, phone and email. Tick **Visible to field staff** only on those your staff should see.
9. Write the **Field notes**: what you need to know on arrival. Whoever has a task for this customer will see them.
10. If needed, write the **Management notes**: they are only shown in the panel.
11. Save.

> **Tip:** field notes accept formatting (headings, lists, bold). A short list —*Keys at the porter's lodge · Doorbell 3rd B · Not before 9:00*— reads better on a phone than a paragraph.

## The customer's name and your staff

A customer's name can be sensitive information: if you work as a subcontractor, for example, the end customer's name together with their contact's phone number is exactly what you should not hand out. That is why it **comes switched off**.

- **Unticked**, staff see in their tasks the instructions, the visible contacts and the documents for workers, but **not the customer's name**.
- **Ticked**, they also see it on the task. The change also reaches tasks that are already open, without having to edit them.

The **task title** is always visible: if you do not want the name to be known, do not put it in the title.

When you mark a contact as **Visible to field staff**, the form reminds you that staff will see their name, role and phone.

---

## The list and the search box

The list shows each customer with their type, branches, how many contacts they have and their status. To find one:

- **Search by name, tax ID or reference**: from three letters.
- **Branch**: only the customers a site serves.
- **Status**: *Active* ones are shown by default; you can also see *Archived* or *Active and archived*.

From each row, **View**, **Edit** or **Archive**.

---

## The customer record

The record has two tabs:

- **Details**: address, contacts, field notes, **Documents**, **Contracts**, **Expenses without a contract** (if there are any) and management notes. At the top, the figures for contacts and branches and, in red, whether any contract expires within 30 days or has already expired.
- **Analysis**: how the work with this customer is going. See [The customer in the analyses](./123-the-customer-in-the-analyses.md).

From the header, **Edit** changes the details and **Customer report** downloads the summary of the work done.

### Change the branches that serve the customer

On the branches figure of the record, press **Edit**, choose the branches and save. A manager can only assign the customer to branches they are in charge of.

---

## Archive a customer

When you stop working with a customer, **archive them** from the list, with the **Archive** button on their row. They stop appearing in the selectors and in the search box, but their history is kept. To bring them back, find them with the **Status** filter set to *Archived*, press **Edit** and change their **Status** to *Active*.

---

## Who can do it

- **Owner and administrator**: all the company's customers.
- **Manager**: sees and manages the customers of their branches.
- **Supervisor**: sees the customers of their branches, without editing them.
- **Worker**: does not see the portfolio; sees in their tasks what you have decided to show.

---

## Common problems

**It will not let me save the address.** You have to pick it from the list of suggestions that appears as you type; an address typed by hand without picking it is not valid. If you do not want to enter an address, leave the field empty.

**The customer does not appear when I create a task.** The customer has to be assigned to the task's branch and be *Active*. Check their branches in the record.

**Staff see the customer's name even though I did not tick the box.** Look at the task title: it is always visible.

**The search box finds nothing.** Type at least three letters, and check that the **Status** filter is not leaving out the archived ones.
