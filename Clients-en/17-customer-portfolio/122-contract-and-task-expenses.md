# Contract and task expenses

Not everything a customer costs is hours. There is a subcontractor, a rental, a trip; and day to day, the car park, the product bought on the spot or the tow truck. If that is not recorded, the contract's margin looks better than it is. In CheckBiz360 those expenses are recorded where they happen —on the contract or on the task—, with their receipt, and they count towards the customer's cost.

---

## What problems it solves

- **A margin that includes everything**, not just labour.
- **Receipts stop getting lost.** Whoever pays uploads the photo from the task, there and then.
- **Nothing counts without your approval.** What staff record stays pending until you approve it or reject it with a reason.
- **The receipt, filed on its own.** On approval, the receipt goes into the customer's documents, visible only to Administration.

Expenses are for the **owner and administrators**: only they see them in the panel, record them and approve them. From the app, the person assigned to a task with a customer can record their own.

---

## Contract expenses

For what is paid out for a whole contract: subcontractors, rentals, travel, allowances.

1. Open the customer record and expand the contract card.
2. In **External expenses**, press **Add expense**.
3. If you have the receipt or invoice, attach it in **Receipt or invoice (optional)**: PDF or JPG, PNG or WEBP image. It is saved in the customer's documents as the expense receipt.
4. Enter the **Description**, the **Receipt total (€, incl. VAT)** and, if you know it, the **Net amount excl. VAT (€)**.
5. Set the **Date** and press **Save expense**.

The expense is approved straight away and counts immediately towards the contract's cost. The card shows the expenses 5 at a time, with **Load more**, and their total.

On a **closed** contract you can still record expenses that arrive late. To change or delete an expense, use **Edit** on the contract: each expense is saved on its own, without waiting for the contract to be saved.

## Expenses from the task

For what is paid out on a specific job. The **Expenses** block appears on the task page when the task has a customer.

### Record it from the panel

1. On the task page, **Expenses** block, press **Add expense**.
2. Attach the receipt if you have it (it is saved in the task's attachments), and fill in **Description**, **Receipt total**, **Net amount excl. VAT** if you know it, and **Date**.
3. Save. It is approved straight away and counts towards the customer's cost and, if the task has a contract, towards the contract's.

### From the app: the assigned person uploads the receipt

1. On the task, in **Files and expenses**, press **Expense**.
2. Take a photo of the receipt with **Camera**, or choose a photo from the **Gallery** or a **PDF**. It cannot be saved without a receipt.
3. Enter the **Description**, the **Receipt total (incl. VAT)** and the **Date**.
4. Press **Save**. The expense is left *Pending approval*.

The person sees on the task the status of each expense (*Pending approval*, *Approved* or *Rejected*, with the reason) and can delete it as long as it has not been approved. A task without a customer does not accept expenses. To record expenses from the app, it must be updated to the latest version.

---

## Approve or reject

What staff record **does not count towards any cost until it is approved**. You can approve it from four places, with the same buttons:

- The task page, **Expenses** block: *N staff expenses are awaiting approval*.
- The contract card, **Expenses awaiting approval**.
- The customer record, **Expenses without a contract**.
- **Home**, **Expenses awaiting approval** alert: it opens all the company's pending expenses so you can decide on them in one go, each with the link to its task.

Next to each pending expense are the **Receipt total** and the **Net amount excl. VAT (€)**, which is what counts towards the cost. Check it and press **Approve**. If the person did not separate the VAT, the expense carries the tag *VAT not separated: check the net amount*: enter the net amount and the tag disappears.

To **Reject**, enter the **Reason**: the person will see it in the app.

On approval, the receipt is copied to the customer's documents as an **Expense receipt**, visible only to **Administration**, with the description, the amount and the date in the title. If the expense is later deleted, the receipt is archived.

---

## The VAT on the receipt

- The **receipt total, including VAT**, is always recorded, which is what appears on the paper.
- What counts towards the cost is the **net amount excluding VAT**. If it is known, it is entered; if not, the total counts and the expense is tagged **VAT not separated** to be reviewed on approval.

---

## Which contract each expense goes to

- An expense on a task **follows its task's contract**.
- If the task has no contract, neither does the expense: it appears under **Expenses without a contract**, in the customer record.

### Expenses without a contract

In the customer record, the **Expenses without a contract** block brings together the expenses of their tasks that do not belong to any contract, with what has been approved and what is pending. They count in the customer's **Analysis** once approved.

If an expense did belong to a contract, press **Assign to contract**: the contracts in force on the day of the expense come first and, after them, the rest marked *not in force*. From the moment you save, the expense counts towards that contract. An expense already assigned can be moved with **Change contract** or left as **No contract**. The receipt stays attached to the expense.

---

## Common problems

**I cannot see the Expenses block on the task.** The task has no customer, or your user is not an owner or administrator.

**An expense from the app does not appear on the contract.** Either it is pending approval, or its task has no contract: look for it under **Expenses without a contract** in the customer record.

**The contract's cost has not changed after recording an expense from the app.** Staff expenses do not count until they are approved.

**The person says they cannot record an expense.** Check that the task has a customer and that their app is up to date.
