# What is the customer portfolio

CheckBiz360 already knows who worked, where, when and on which task. The **customer portfolio** adds **for whom**. With each task tied to its customer, you can answer questions that usually go unanswered today:

> *What have we done for this customer this month? How many hours have we spent on them? Is it paying off?*

The portfolio is included in CheckBiz360: there is nothing to subscribe to or activate. It is in the side menu, under **Customers**.

---

## What problems it solves

| What happens today | How the portfolio solves it |
|---|---|
| The customer's details are scattered across emails, spreadsheets and the memory of whoever handles them. | Each customer has a record with their address, contacts, notes and documents. |
| Whoever goes to do the work arrives without knowing where the keys are or whom to call. | The task carries the instructions and contacts you decide to show, and the person sees them in the app. |
| Nobody knows how much work has been done for each customer. | Each task says which customer it belongs to, and the record adds up tasks, hours and people, month by month. |
| Nobody knows whether a contract leaves a margin until it is too late. | The contract compares its amount with what its tasks and expenses are costing, and warns you before it goes over. |
| The summary of the work done has to be put together by hand to send to the customer. | The **Customer report** comes out in PDF or Excel, with the tasks, the hours and the evidence photos. |
| Receipts for what is paid out (a car park, a purchase) get lost. | The assigned person uploads the receipt from the task in the app; you approve it and it counts towards the customer's cost. |

---

## How it is organised

- **Customer.** A company or a person: the residents' association, the person cared for at home, the developer of the building work. It has its address, contacts, notes and documents.
- **Branches that serve it.** Each customer is assigned to one or more branches (or projects, depending on what your company calls its sites). When a task is created in that branch, that customer can be chosen.
- **Tasks.** Each task can belong to a customer. It can use the customer's address as its work site and carry the customer's details to whoever goes to do the work.
- **Contracts.** A customer can have several contracts at the same time, each with its amount, its hours and its term. Each task says which contract it belongs to, or that it belongs to none.
- **Expenses.** What is paid out for a customer: for the contract (a subcontractor, a rental) or for a task (a car park, material bought for the job).

---

## Where everything is

- **Customers** (side menu): the list, the search box and each customer's record, with two tabs: **Details** and **Analysis**.
- **Tasks**: the customer and the contract are chosen in the task form, and the task page shows the customer card and its expenses.
- **Home**: the **Customers** block and the customer and contract alerts.
- **Reports** (side menu): the **Customer report** button.
- **App**: the **Additional details** card on the task and expenses with receipts. Ask your staff to keep the app updated to the latest version.

## Who sees what

| | Owner and administrator | Manager | Supervisor | Worker |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| See customers | All | Those of their branches | Those of their branches | Does not see the portfolio |
| Create and edit customers | Yes | In their branches | No | No |
| Contracts and their hours | Yes | Yes, without amounts | Yes, without amounts | No |
| Amount, cost, margin and expenses | Yes | No | No | No |
| Documents marked "Administration" | Yes, from the panel | No | No | No |
| What the task carries for its customer | — | — | — | Instructions, visible contacts and documents for workers; the customer's name only if you authorise it |

Anyone who signs in through the app is just another employee, even the owner: from the app nobody sees amounts, contracts or "Administration" documents.

## Before you start: what each position costs

For profitability to come out (in contracts and in the customer's **Analysis** tab), the platform needs to know what each hour worked costs. That figure is each person's **hourly rate** at their branch, the one set when linking the employee to the site (see [Create and manage employees](../02-employees/06-create-and-manage-employees.md)).

If someone is missing it, their hours are shown but do not count towards the cost, and the platform says so: *Some hours have no rate: the cost falls short*. It is worth checking this before trusting a margin.

If a person does different kinds of work at different rates, they can have [several jobs](../02-employees/140-several-jobs-for-the-same-person.md) and choose which one they go with on each task; and what they are paid on top for a particular task is recorded as [supplements](../02-employees/141-supplements.md). Both go into the contract's cost.

## Getting started in five steps

1. Check that people have their hourly rate at each branch.
2. Add your customers, with their address and the branches that serve them. See [Add customers](./118-add-customers.md).
3. Upload their records and, if you have them, create their contracts. See [Customer documents](./120-customer-documents.md) and [Contracts and profitability](./121-contracts-and-profitability.md).
4. When creating tasks, choose the customer. See [The customer on the task](./119-the-customer-on-the-task.md).
5. After a few weeks, look at the customer's **Analysis** tab and the **Customers** block on **Home**. See [The customer in the analyses](./123-the-customer-in-the-analyses.md).
