# The customer in the analyses

With tasks tied to their customer, CheckBiz360 can tell the story of the relationship with each one: how much work is done for them, whether it is delivered on time, who serves them and whether they are profitable. And it warns you when something goes wrong: tasks that were not done, contract hours running out, a contract expiring.

It all comes from the tasks, check-ins and contracts you already have: there is nothing to configure. For cost and margin to come out, people need to have their **hourly rate** (see [What is the customer portfolio](./117-what-is-the-customer-portfolio.md#before-you-start-what-each-position-costs)).

---

## What problems it solves

- **Knowing which customers make you money** and which cost you more than they pay.
- **Seeing what is going wrong before the customer does**: tasks not done, delays, fewer hours than last month.
- **Sending the customer what has been done for them**, with proof, without putting it together by hand.
- **Not depending on a single person**: seeing whether all of a customer's work falls on one person.

---

## The "Analysis" tab of the record

In **Customers**, open a customer's record and press the **Analysis** tab. A manager only sees what belongs to their branches, and the tab says so at the top: *Includes the tasks of: …*.

### What to look at

A short list of signals from recent months, each marked **Important**, **Keep an eye on** or **Good to know**. For example:

- Tasks not done in the last 30 days, or a rising number of cancellations.
- Fewer hours than the average of the three previous months, or fewer tasks scheduled than usual.
- A negative margin or one falling compared with previous months.
- A contract's hours exceeded, or about to run out before its end at the current pace.
- A contract ending with no other one after it.
- Tasks finishing late more often than usual, or taking longer than planned.
- A single person doing most of the customer's hours.

If there is nothing: *Nothing out of the ordinary in the last 3 months*.

### Profitability

Only for the owner and administrators. The verdict for the last 3 months —**Profitable**, **Not profitable**, *No contract amount* (we know what it costs, not what it brings in) or *No work*— and its figures: **Revenue**, **Cost**, **Margin**, **Cost per hour** and **Revenue per hour**, with a chart of revenue and cost month by month. The cost includes approved expenses. If there are hours from people without a rate, it says so: those hours do not count towards the cost.

### Forecast

- **At month end**: what has been done this month plus what is still scheduled, in hours (and in cost and margin for the owner and administrators).
- **Next 30 days**: how many tasks are scheduled and how many hours they add up to.
- **Contracts**: for each active contract, the hours done out of those estimated, those left and, at the pace of the last 30 days, when they would run out.

### Relationship, service and team

- **Relationship**: customer since when, how many months with work, the last 3 months against the previous 3, and the **Month by month** view of tasks (scheduled, done, not done, cancelled) and hours (worked against planned). **This month** is compared with last month at this point.
- **Service on time and as agreed**: tasks done, on time and late, average delay, not done, cancelled, actual duration against planned, with evidence, rejected at validation and average rating.
- **Team**: how many people work for the customer, what share of the hours each one does and how much of their time they devote to the customer.

The analysis needs the customer to have had tasks for at least a month; before that, it says so.

## The customer report

A summary of what has been done for the customer in a period, ready to send to them.

1. In the customer record, press **Customer report** (or, in **Reports**, the **Customer report** button and choose the customer).
2. Choose the **Period**: *Last month* or *Other dates*.
3. Choose the **Format**: *PDF* or *Excel (XLSX)*.
4. Press **Download**.

The report is also saved in **Reports**.

| | PDF | Excel |
|---|---|---|
| Header | Your company, the customer, their address and the period | — |
| Summary | Tasks done out of those scheduled, hours worked, people and evidence | — |
| One row per task | Date, task, who did it, start and end, hours, status and evidence; cancelled ones, separately at the end | The same rows, to work with |
| Photos | The evidence photos, small, under their task | — |
| People | — | Sheet with each person's hours |
| Material | If you have inventory, what was used on each task | If you have inventory, material sheet |

The report **contains no costs, prices or margins**: it is designed to be sent to the customer.

## The "Customers" block on Home

On **Home**, the **Customers** block shows the customers with the most hours this month, compared with the same stretch of last month: hours worked, what share of the hours of all your customers each one takes, and whether they are going up or down. One sentence sums up whether the work is concentrated on one customer or spread out, and another how many customers have tasks not done this month. **See all customers** takes you to the list.

It appears when there are tasks with a customer in the month.

---

## Customer and contract alerts

On **Home**, in the list of alerts, with a **View** link that takes you to where it is fixed:

| Alert | What has happened |
|---|---|
| **Customer tasks not done** | A customer has tasks from the last 7 days that were not done. |
| **Fewer hours for a customer** | A customer has had considerably fewer hours this month than last month at this point. |
| **Contract over plan** | An active contract has more hours done than estimated. |
| **Contract ending** | An active contract ends in 30 days or less. |
| **Expenses awaiting approval** | There are expenses recorded by staff from the app waiting for your approval. **View** opens the list so you can decide on them right there. |

A manager sees the customer alerts for their branches; contract and expense alerts are for the owner and administrators.

These alerts can also arrive by email, in the morning summary. They are switched on or off in **Tools** → **Email alerts** (see [Email alerts](../09-notifications/133-email-alerts.md)).

## Common problems

**The Analysis tab says there is not enough data.** The customer needs at least one month with tasks.

**Profitability says "No contract amount".** The customer has no contracts with an amount. Create the contract with its amount.

**The cost seems low.** Hourly rates are missing: the profitability block says how many hours have no price.

**The Customers block does not appear on Home.** There are no tasks with a customer this month.

**The report comes out empty.** The customer has no tasks in that period. Check that the tasks have the customer chosen.
