# Automatic reports

Automatic reports are scheduled jobs that generate and send a report by email to the configured recipients, at whatever frequency the manager defines (daily, weekly or monthly). Their purpose is for managers and owners to receive attendance, cost or anomaly data periodically without having to go into the back office manually to request it.

This feature will be especially useful for teams needing recurring operational tracking or who have to share consolidated information regularly with accountancy firms or other external parties.
