# Multi-branch global view

Whoever runs several sites needs to see them all at once before going into any of them: at 9:30, in under a minute, know which shops have people who have not clocked in yet, which are fully staffed and where something needs attention now. In CheckBiz360 that overview is not a separate screen: it is three places, each for one question.

## Who it is for

Those who supervise at scale: operations directors with several shops, people managers in multi-site companies, area managers. They are the people with owner or administrator access to the company panel, who see every site.

## Where each overview lives

| Question | Where | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **How is today going?** | **Home**, the panel's front page | Site by site: expected, in, not clocked in, late, today's tasks and cost. The alerts that need attention at the very top. And the recent weeks with the same figures. It refreshes on its own. |
| **How did the month go, and who stands out?** | **Operations panel** | The analysis by period: reliability, site effect, dependency and cost, by branch and by person, with the assistant's narrative. |
| **How many people do I have each day at each site?** | **Schedules → Availability** | Coverage by branch and day, who is missing and why, and export to Excel. |

The three are explained in their own articles: [The home page: today, alerts and recent weeks](../07-operational-intelligence/112-the-home-page-today-alerts-and-recent-weeks.md), [The Operations panel](../07-operational-intelligence/56-dashboards.md) and [Availability board](../04-calendar-and-schedules/98-availability-board.md).

## Comparing sites

The home page sorts sites by what needs most attention and shows the week-by-week figures per site. The Operations panel compares sites over a period: cost, punctuality and reliability, each site against its own history. For cost comparisons to be reliable, each person needs their rate where they are linked to their site: when it is missing, the home page warns with *missing rates* and the panel marks the figure as partial.

## What there is not

There is no separate global dashboard with filters by region, no consolidated PDF export, and no assigning an alert to someone. The home page alerts are read and take you to the screen where each thing is fixed; reports are generated from **Reports** by branch and period.
