# Delete documents and free up space

Archiving a document removes it from view, but the file stays and keeps taking up space. **Delete** really removes it: the file, the signed document and the signature record disappear, and the space is freed immediately. What does not disappear is the record: which document it was, who received or signed it and when, and who deleted it and why.

---

## What it solves

- **Freeing up space** when your company approaches or exceeds the 2 GB included, and no longer paying for documents you do not need.
- **Cleaning up in one go**: several documents can be deleted at once from the list.
- **Keeping the proof**: if someone makes a claim, there is a record that the document existed, of who received it and of who deleted it.

---

## Archive or delete

| | Archive | Delete |
|---|---|---|
| The document is no longer shown in the app | Yes | Yes |
| The file is kept | Yes | No: it is erased |
| Frees up space | No | Yes, immediately |
| Can be undone | Yes | **No** |
| Who can | Whoever manages documents | Only the owner and administrators |

If in doubt, archive. Delete when you know you will not need the file.

---

## Deleting a document

1. In **Document Management**, open the document.
2. Click **Delete**, next to **Archive**.
3. The window says how much space is freed and, where needed, warns you in red:
   - if someone **signed it or acknowledged receipt**, who: deleting it also erases the signed document and the signature record, and only the record of who received it and when remains;
   - if it is a person's **data protection information sheet**: without it you will not have proof of having informed them, and they will appear in the alerts again;
   - if it is a **payslip or a contract**: they may have to be kept by law. Make sure you have a copy.
4. Write the **reason** if you wish (it is optional) and tick **I understand this cannot be undone**.
5. Confirm. The panel says how much space has been freed.

The warnings do not prevent deletion: they tell you what you lose so you can decide with the facts in front of you.

## Deleting several at once

1. In the **Document Management** list, select the documents.
2. Click **Delete selected**. The window summarises how many there are, how much space is freed and how many have a signature or are payslips or contracts.
3. Tick **I understand this cannot be undone** and confirm.

If any cannot be deleted, the panel says so and the rest are deleted anyway. Try again with the remaining ones.

---

## What remains of a deleted document

It appears in the list with the **Deleted** status (you can filter it with the *Deleted* status). Its record no longer has a download, but it keeps:

- the document's name, type and size;
- who received it, acknowledged receipt or signed it, and when;
- who deleted it, when, with what **Reason for deletion** and how much **Space freed**.

Staff no longer see it in the app.

The company's used storage goes down immediately: you can check it in **Entity → Subscriptions**, in the **Storage** block.

---

## Common problems

**I do not see the Delete button.** Only the owner and administrators have it. Whoever manages documents with another role can archive.

**I deleted a document by mistake.** It cannot be recovered from the panel. If you have a copy of the file, upload it again. If it was a signed document, the signature is not recovered: it will have to be sent for signature again.

**I deleted an information sheet and the person appears in the alerts again.** That is expected: there is no longer proof that it was given to them. Send it to them again. See [Data protection information sheet](../12-regulatory-compliance/137-data-protection-information-sheet.md).
