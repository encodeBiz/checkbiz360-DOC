# Collective agreements

Your company's collective agreements are in **Document Management**, **Agreements** button. For each one, its salary tables are kept, **one per year** the gazette publishes, together with each year's working hours. This is what the reading of the employment contract uses to propose the hourly rate (see [What the employment contract says](138-what-the-employment-contract-says.md)).

Agreements belong to **your company**: you see the ones you referenced, and no other company sees yours. A company does not have "its agreement": it can have several, for different people. That is why the agreement is decided contract by contract, and what is managed here is the list of those available.

---

## What it solves

- **No looking up the tables every time.** The agreement and its tables are read once from the official gazette and serve for every contract.
- **Every year at once.** A gazette usually publishes the tables for two or three years. All of them are kept, each with its working hours, and each contract uses the one for its year.
- **Knowing which year the figure is from.** A 2025 salary applied to a 2026 contract is flagged.
- **Adding yours.** With the link to the gazette or the PDF, your company uses it from that moment.

---

## The list

| Column | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Agreement** | The official name. |
| **Scope** | National, regional, provincial or company, with the territory. |
| **Tables** | The years with a table: *Tables for 2025, 2026 and 2027*. *No tables* if they have not been read yet; *Reading the document…* while it is being read; *The document could not be read* if it failed. |
| **Status** | **Under review: you can already use it**: you referenced it and you are already using it; support will review it. **Reviewed by support**: support checked the tables against the gazette. **Rejected**, with the reason: support did not accept it (a document that was not the agreement, a link that was not official). |

Pressing an agreement opens its **detail**: one tab per year and, for each one, the full-time hours, the link to the document and the table with its rows: the category, the group or level, the **positions** the agreement assigns to each group, the amount with its period and payments, and the block if the agreement has several tables. It is the same you see when you press **Choose row** in the reading of a contract.

When a contract does not cite an agreement or yours is not there, the **legal minimum** applies (Workers' Statute and minimum wage): the legal maximum hours and the minimum wage as a floor.

## Referencing an agreement

If the agreement of a contract is not on the list, add it:

1. Press **Reference agreement**.
2. **Agreement name**, as the gazette publishes it: "Collective agreement for the cleaning of buildings and premises of Madrid".
3. **Scope** and, if it is regional or provincial, the **Autonomous community** or the **province**. The province comes proposed from your company's address.
4. **Document**: the **link to the official gazette** (BOE, regional or provincial gazette) or the **agreement PDF** (up to 10 MB). Only gazettes and official sites are accepted: a salary figure is defended with whoever publishes it.
5. Press **Reference**.

There is no year to enter: it is read from the document. The screen confirms *We have received the agreement* and, within a few minutes, the **Tables** column goes from *Reading the document…* to the years the document contained. From that moment you can choose it when uploading a contract or in the **Agreement** block of a reading. Support then reviews the rows against the gazette and, if it approves them, the agreement moves to **Reviewed by support**.

You can also reference from wherever you are: the **Agreement** block of a contract reading and the **Collective agreement (optional)** selector in the upload form have **Reference another agreement**, which opens the same form without taking you out of the record.

If the agreement is already on your list and you give it a new document (the gazette with next year's tables), it is not duplicated: the new years are added to the one you already had, and the reading tells you it is the same agreement.

> **The PDF counts as one of your company's documents.** If you upload it as a file, it stays in your document management, visible only to Administration, and takes up space like any other document. The link to the gazette takes up nothing.

## What is in a table

For each agreement and year, the **full-time salary table** is kept: one row per category, group or level, with the base salary and the number of payments (or the annual amount), and that year's **full-time hours**: hours per year and per week.

When the table goes by **groups** ("Group 1", "Group 3"…), each row also carries the **positions** the agreement assigns to that group in its professional classification article ("Senior operations technician", "Senior account technician"…). That way a contract that states the occupation and not the group finds its row.

Some agreements include **several tables** for the same year: one per scope (in care for dependent people, home care and care homes), per area or per type of establishment, sometimes with the same category repeated with different amounts and hours. In that case each row states its **block** and, if it has them, that block's hours, and the reading of the contract chooses the block by the contract's sector and occupation. If it is not sure, it asks you to choose the row.

## Common problems

**The list is empty.** Your company has not referenced any agreement yet. Reference the one that applies to your contracts.

**The document could not be read.** The link was not to the agreement document (a search page, an index), the PDF was an image with no text, or the document had no salary table. Reference it again with the direct link to the PDF or to the gazette text.

**The agreement shows as "Rejected".** The reason is next to it. Most often: the link was not from an official gazette or the document was not the agreement. Reference it again with the official source.

**My agreement is there but without this person's category.** The rows are read from the gazette; if one is missing, let support know, with the agreement and the category. In the meantime, in the reading of the contract use **Type the price by hand**.

**The tables are from a year earlier than the contract.** The most recent one available is used and the reading warns you. When the gazette publishes the new ones, reference the agreement again with that document: the new years are added.
