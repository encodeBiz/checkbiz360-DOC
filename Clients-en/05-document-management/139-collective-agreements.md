# Collective agreements

The collective agreements that apply to your company are in **Document Management**, **Agreements** button. For each one, its salary table and its working hours are kept, with the **year** they correspond to. This is what the reading of the employment contract uses to propose the hourly rate (see [What the employment contract says](138-what-the-employment-contract-says.md)).

A company does not have "its agreement": it can have several, for different people. That is why the agreement is decided contract by contract, and what is managed here is the list of those available.

---

## What it solves

- **No looking up the tables every time.** The agreement and its tables are read once from the official gazette and serve for every contract.
- **Knowing which year the figure is from.** Each agreement says which year its tables are from. A 2025 salary applied to a 2026 contract is flagged.
- **Adding yours if it is missing.** With the link to the gazette or the PDF, your company uses it from that moment.

---

## Before you start

The list depends on your company's **activity** and on the **provinces** of its address and its branches: a cleaning company in Madrid sees the national cleaning agreements, those of the Community of Madrid and those of its province, not the hospitality ones from Seville.

If the company does not have its activity set, the screen says so (*To use agreements, first set your company's activity*) and the **Set the activity** button takes you to **Organisation**, *What your company does*. See [Organisation configuration](../08-branches-and-organisation/61-organisation-settings.md).

---

## The list

| Column | What it says |
|---|---|
| **Agreement** | The official name. Those your company contributed carry *contributed by your company*. |
| **Scope** | National, regional, provincial or company, with the territory. |
| **Tables** | *2025 tables* is the most recent year available. *No tables* if they have not been read yet; *2025 table in preparation* while it is being read; *the 2025 table could not be read* if it failed. |
| **Status** | **In the catalogue**: reviewed and available to every company in that activity. **Contributed by your company, under review**: you referenced it and you are already using it; support will review it. **Rejected**, with the reason: support did not accept it (a document that was not the agreement, a link that was not official). |

The **Legal minimum (Workers' Statute and minimum wage)** entry is always there. It is what applies when a contract does not cite an agreement or yours is not there: the legal maximum hours and the minimum wage as a floor.

## Referencing an agreement

If the agreement of a contract is not on the list, add it:

1. Press **Reference agreement**.
2. **Agreement name**, as the gazette publishes it: "Collective agreement for the cleaning of buildings and premises of Madrid".
3. **Scope** and, if it is regional or provincial, the **Autonomous community** or the **province**. The province comes proposed from your company's address.
4. **Table year**: the year the salaries correspond to, not the year of publication.
5. **Document**: the **link to the official gazette** (BOE, regional or provincial gazette) or the **agreement PDF** (up to 10 MB). Only gazettes and official sites are accepted: a salary figure is defended with whoever publishes it.
6. Press **Reference**.

The screen confirms *We have received the agreement* and, within a few minutes, the **Tables** column goes from *in preparation* to the table year. From that moment you can choose it when uploading a contract or in the **Agreement** block of a reading. Support then reviews the rows against the gazette; if it approves it, it moves to **In the catalogue** and the other companies in your activity see it too.

A **company**-scope agreement is seen only by yours.

If the agreement is already there for that year, the form says so (*That agreement is already in the catalogue for that year*). If you have several agreements under review, wait for support to review them before referencing more.

> **The PDF counts as one of your company's documents.** If you upload it as a file, it stays in your document management, visible only to Administration, and takes up space like any other document. The link to the gazette takes up nothing.

## What is in a table

For each agreement and year, the **full-time salary table** is kept: one row per category, group or level, with the base salary and the number of payments (or the annual amount), and the agreement's **full-time hours**: hours per year and per week. This is what you see when you press **Choose row** in the reading of a contract.

Some agreements include **several tables**: one per scope (in care for dependent people, home care and care homes), per area or per type of establishment, sometimes with the same category repeated with different amounts and hours. In that case each row states its **block** and, if it has them, that block's hours, and the reading of the contract chooses the block by the contract's sector and occupation. If it is not sure, it asks you to choose the row.

When new tables are published for an agreement that is already there, reference it again with the **new year**: the previous table is kept for that year's contracts.

## Common problems

**The list is empty.** Either the company does not have its activity set, or there are no agreements for that activity yet. Set the activity in **Organisation** and reference the agreement that applies to your contracts.

**The table could not be read.** The link was not to the agreement document (a search page, an index), the PDF was an image with no text, or the document had no salary table. Reference it again with the direct link to the PDF or to the gazette text.

**The agreement shows as "Rejected".** The reason is next to it. Most often: the link was not from an official gazette or the document was not the agreement. Reference it again with the official source.

**My agreement is there but without this person's category.** The rows are read from the gazette; if one is missing, let support know, with the agreement and the category. In the meantime, in the reading of the contract use **Type the price by hand**.

**The tables are from a year earlier than the contract.** The most recent one available is used and the reading warns you. When the gazette publishes the new ones, reference them with that year.
