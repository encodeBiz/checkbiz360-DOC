# Who sees each document: Administration, personal documents and the app

A customer contract, a quote or management papers do not need to be seen by site managers, and one person's payslip does not need to be seen by a colleague. This article explains who sees each document, from where, and how to mark one so that only the owner and administrators can open it.

---

## The rule in three sentences

1. **Company and branch documents** are seen by whoever belongs to them and has their role ticked in **Visible to**.
2. **A person's document** (*Employee* scope) is seen only by that person and, from the panel, by the owner and administrators. No colleague, whatever their role.
3. **The app is for staff.** Whoever signs in through the app, even the owner, sees the same as any other worker: their personal documents and their company's or branch's documents marked for **Worker**.

---

## "Visible to": the four levels

When uploading a company or branch document, **Visible to** has four checkboxes:

| Checkbox | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Worker** | Staff, also from the app. |
| **Supervisor** | Supervisors. |
| **Manager** | Managers. |
| **Administration** | The owner and administrators, from the panel. |

### Only "Administration"

If you tick **only Administration**, the document:

- is opened by the owner and administrators, **only from the panel**;
- **does not appear in anyone's app**, not even the owner's;
- is not seen by the branch manager or supervisor, not even in the panel;
- notifies nobody when uploaded and does not allow acknowledgement of receipt (the checkbox is disabled).

The form reminds you: *Only the owner and administrators open it, from the panel. It does not appear in the app.*

It is the right level for customer contracts and quotes, financial documents and management papers. A customer's contracts and quotes are created already marked this way.

> **Warning:** if you mark a customer's administrative document for supervisors or workers, the panel warns you in red: they will see it from that customer's tasks, with its prices and conditions.

On a person's documents (*Employee* scope) the **Administration** checkbox does not appear: only that person and Administration see them already.

---

## From the panel and from the app

| Who | In the panel | In the app |
|---|---|---|
| Owner and administrators | All documents, including each person's personal ones and the Administration-only ones | Their personal documents and those marked for **Worker** |
| Manager and supervisor | Company or branch documents marked for their role | Their personal documents and those marked for their role |
| Worker | — | Their personal documents and those marked for **Worker** |

Archived and deleted documents do not appear in the app.

---

## Common problems

**I am the owner and in the app I do not see a document that I do see in the panel.** That is intended: in the app you see the same as staff. Open it from the panel.

**A manager says they cannot see a document from their branch.** Check **Visible to**: if only **Administration** is ticked, or **Manager** is not ticked, they do not see it.

**A site manager cannot see the payslip of someone on their team.** That is intended: a person's document is seen only by them and Administration.
