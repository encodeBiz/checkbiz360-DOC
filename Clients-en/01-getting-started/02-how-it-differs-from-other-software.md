# How it differs from other clocking software

Most companies looking for a time-tracking solution start from the same basic need: knowing when each employee arrives and leaves. Traditional solutions meet that need well enough for a while, until the company grows, opens new branches or faces a labour inspection and discovers that the data it holds is not sufficient.

CheckBiz360 is designed for the moment when simple clocking in stops being enough. It does not limit itself to recording attendance; it builds operational intelligence on top of every record and puts it at the service of the people making decisions.

## What traditional solutions offer

Conventional clocking solutions —whether physical terminals, basic web applications or integrations into HR systems— share a common approach: record the entry and the exit, calculate the hours worked and export a list. They are recording tools, not analysis tools.

Their most common limitations are:

- They do not detect anomalies automatically. Somebody has to review the records manually to find problems.
- They do not calculate costs. Connecting attendance with real spend requires additional work outside the system.
- They do not compare between units. There is no way to know whether one branch is performing better than another without exporting and cross-referencing data by hand.
- Change traceability is partial or non-existent. If somebody modifies a record, there is not always a trace of who did it or why.
- Regulatory compliance requires additional work. Reports for RDL 8/2019 have to be prepared separately.

## What CheckBiz360 adds

CheckBiz360 starts from the attendance record and turns it into the entry point to an operational intelligence system. Every clock-in triggers a set of processes: context validation, deviation analysis, cost calculation and real-time indicator updates.

This means that when a supervisor opens their branch dashboard in the morning, they do not see a list of entries and exits; they see an operational summary: who is inside the perimeter, who is arriving late, how much the shift is costing at that moment and whether there is any anomaly that needs their attention.

## The differential value in practice

### Anomaly detection with no manual intervention

A traditional system will hand you a list of 200 records at the end of the day. You will have to review them one by one to detect whether somebody clocked in from outside the branch, whether there was an entry with no exit or whether an employee is accumulating recurring late arrivals.

CheckBiz360 identifies those anomalies the moment they happen and classifies them by severity. You receive a notification and can act immediately, without reviewing the whole list.

### Real costs, not estimated hours

Labour cost is not just the base salary. It includes overtime, night-shift premiums, public holidays and unjustified absences. CheckBiz360 connects each attendance record with the employee's salary profile and calculates the real cost of the shift, the week or the month, per employee, per branch or across the whole organisation.

### Traceability that stands up to an audit

When the Labour Inspectorate requests the working-time record, presenting a list of hours is not enough. You need to demonstrate that the records are intact and that no subsequent modification has altered the original data without leaving a trace. CheckBiz360 logs every change with the user's identity, a timestamp and a reason, forming a complete audit history.

### Document management and tasks on the same platform

Traditional solutions stop at time tracking. CheckBiz360 also integrates the management of employment documents —contracts, payslips, certificates— and the assignment of operational tasks. All on the same platform, with no need for additional tools.

## Who CheckBiz360 is ideal for

CheckBiz360 offers a greater return the more complex the company's operation is. It is particularly suited to:

- Companies with between 10 and 500 employees operating across several sites or with distributed teams.
- Organisations that need to demonstrate compliance with RDL 8/2019 to labour inspections.
- Operations managers who want to make decisions based on data, not intuition.
- Companies where labour cost is a significant line item that needs to be monitored precisely.

> **Note:** If your company has fewer than 10 employees and operates from a single site with no need for advanced analysis, a basic clocking solution may be enough at an early stage. CheckBiz360 is designed to scale as the organisation grows.
