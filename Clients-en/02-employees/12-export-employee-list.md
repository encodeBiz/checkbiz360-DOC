# Export the employee list

This article explains how to get your headcount data out of the platform and what alternatives exist while direct export of the directory is not available.

---

## Feature status

> **Status: Planned**
> This feature is part of the roadmap and is not available yet in the current version.

The back office **does not currently have a button to export the employee list to CSV**. The **Employees** list is viewed, filtered and searched on screen, but does not generate a downloadable file.

**Importing** employees from CSV, on the other hand, is available: see below.

---

## What you can export today

| Need | Available route |
|---|---|
| Hours worked, attendance and absences per employee and period | **Attendance report**, in PDF or Excel (XLSX) |
| Tasks for the period with their status and compliance | **Task report**, in Excel (XLSX) |
| Evidence of the working-time record for an inspection | **Regulatory compliance report** |

Both reports include the employee's identification, so an attendance export covering a wide period lets you reconstruct the list of active people and their reference details.

See [Export data](../11-reports-and-exports/78-export-to-excel-and-csv.md) for the detail of each report.

---

## Import employees from CSV

The opposite direction is covered. If you are coming from another system or need to create many people at once, you can upload a CSV file with your employees' details and map its columns to the platform's fields during the import process.

This is the recommended route when going live with a large headcount, rather than creating the profiles one by one.

---

## If you need the complete directory

For a complete extraction of the employee directory —for example, for an audit, a system migration or the exercise of the right to data portability— write to **hola@encodebiz.com** to request it. The support team can provide the extraction.

---

## GDPR considerations

Any file that leaves the platform contains employees' personal data (name, email, identity document) and becomes your company's responsibility:

- **Secure storage.** Keep the files in locations with restricted access, not in uncontrolled shared folders.
- **Protected transmission.** If you send the file to your accountancy firm, use an encrypted channel or protect it with a password.
- **Minimisation.** Extract only the period and the fields needed for each purpose.
- **Retention.** Delete the file once its purpose has been fulfilled, unless there is a legal obligation to retain it.

> **Warning:** CheckBiz360 safeguards the data inside the platform with the security measures required by the regulations. Once exported, the security of those files is your company's responsibility.
